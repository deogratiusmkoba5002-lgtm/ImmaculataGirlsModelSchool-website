WHO WE ARE

About Immaculata Girls' Model Secondary School

Immaculata Girls' Model Secondary School is a Catholic girls' boarding school located in Fukayosi, Bagamoyo District, Tanzania. Established in 2024, the school is committed to providing quality education, strong moral values and holistic formation that prepares young women to become confident, responsible and future leaders.

OUR FOUNDATION

Our History

Immaculata Girls' Model Secondary School Fukayosi is a girls' boarding school owned and managed by the Sisters of the Immaculate Heart of Mary Mother of Christ. It was established on January 2024 under the leadership of Rev. Mother Mary claud Oguh and sustained by Rev. Mother Maureen Akabuogu

SCHOOL BUILDING (FRONT VIEW)

The school was started by Rev. Sister Miriam Angela ILonuba as its pioneer principal and graciously assisted by Rev. Sr Maureen Geraldine Udonu as its pioneer bursar

ADMINISTRATION BLOCK

I.G.M.S.S. Witnessed a humble beginning at Mr Oscar's garden in Fukayosi, with a handful of students. Immaculata Girls' Model secondary school is committed to providing excellent education and Morality to her students. After one year of its inception, the school shifted from its temporary site of Oscar garden to the permanent site of the school building. Immaculata Girls' Model Secondary school was established to be a role Model and Unique school in its conduct. The school has the vision of providing holistic formation to Girls' to develop well rounded confident and responsible individuals who aspire to achieve their full potential. Also, provision of positive, safe and stimulated environment for her students to enjoy their learning, achieve their potentials and become independent life – long learners. Immaculata Girls' Model Secondary school is located at Fukayosi town, at the back of Oscar Lodge. The bus station is called Oscar bus Station. The school is about thirty miles from Bagamoyo town.

SCHOOL COMPOUND

In the spirit of hard work, the School Offers all the crucial "O" level subject like; Science subjects, Business subjects and Art subjects. The school fees is a Affordable and paid in installments.As one of the criteria of Mission School, we train decent girls and leaders of tomorrow.
